Privacy & analytics

Updated 14 November 2026

English

This personal CV website uses Umami Cloud for aggregate website analytics without analytics cookies. It measures page visits, LinkedIn campaigns, CV download clicks, certification links, document views and contact actions. Google Analytics is no longer loaded on this website; previously collected GA4 data remains in the owner's Google Analytics account.

The analytics service receives page and referral information, campaign labels, device information, approximate location and interaction events. The website does not call Umami's identify function, send visitor names or email addresses, collect form contents, or record sessions. Custom event data is restricted to document identifiers, action types and page numbers. Read Umami's privacy policy and tracker documentation. The Hobby plan retains analytics history for up to six months.

Your browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal is respected. You can also disable analytics below. This stores only a fixed opt-out preference in this browser, not a tracking identifier. A previous decline of this site's Google Analytics is honored as an opt-out. Old Google Analytics cookies accessible to the website are removed. Disabling collection does not delete records already received by the analytics provider.

Bahasa Indonesia

Website CV pribadi ini menggunakan Umami Cloud untuk statistik agregat tanpa cookie analitik. Pengukuran mencakup kunjungan halaman, kampanye LinkedIn, klik unduh CV, tautan sertifikasi, pembukaan dokumen, dan tindakan kontak. Google Analytics tidak lagi dimuat di website ini; data GA4 yang sudah terkumpul tetap berada di akun Google Analytics pemilik.

Layanan analitik menerima informasi halaman dan rujukan, penanda kampanye, perangkat, perkiraan lokasi, serta event interaksi. Website tidak menggunakan fungsi identify Umami, mengirim nama atau alamat email pengunjung, mengambil isi formulir, maupun merekam sesi. Data event khusus dibatasi pada identitas dokumen, jenis tindakan, dan nomor halaman. Baca kebijakan privasi Umami dan dokumentasi tracker. Paket Hobby menyimpan riwayat analitik hingga enam bulan.

Sinyal Do Not Track atau Global Privacy Control pada browser dihormati. Analitik juga dapat dinonaktifkan melalui tombol di bawah. Pilihan ini hanya menyimpan preferensi penolakan tetap pada browser, bukan identitas pelacakan. Penolakan Google Analytics sebelumnya pada website ini tetap dihormati sebagai pilihan untuk tidak dilacak. Cookie Google Analytics lama yang dapat diakses website dihapus. Menonaktifkan pengumpulan tidak menghapus catatan yang sudah diterima penyedia analitik.
